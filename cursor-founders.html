The Journey

2022

Four friends leave school and start Anysphere, the company behind Cursor, with a bold belief: AI will fundamentally reshape how software is written.

2023

Cursor launches as an AI-first code editor built on VS Code. Developers instantly fall in love with its deep context awareness and tab-completion superpowers.

2024

Cursor reaches millions of users worldwide. Major funding rounds value Anysphere at over $2.5 billion, cementing its place as one of the fastest-growing dev tools ever.

2025 →

The mission continues — building the IDE of the future where AI and human creativity are inseparable.
